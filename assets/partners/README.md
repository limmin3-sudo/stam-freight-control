# Partner portraits

`aide-portrait.webp` and `navigator.jpg` are the previously approved background portraits. The guide's full-body artwork remains separate.

`broker-portrait.webp` is a new AI-assisted portrait expanding the existing broker's Pyro setting and worn jacket/scarf into a head-to-thigh composition. `broker.jpg` is retained as the earlier source.

`director-portrait.webp` is the new AI-assisted tutorial Flight Director portrait, based on the selected silver-haired character reserved in PR14. It uses a bright architectural flight-academy reception lobby, a white V-neck officer shirt, dark skirt and hands gently clasped in front. It is independent of the hauling partner voices and does not replace the operator. The user's final four-card screenshot approved this version; later exaggerated-body concepts are not used.

Both new portraits have opaque backgrounds and proportional WebP conversions. Their generated PNG originals remain outside the public build. These are fictional illustrations, not official Star Citizen assets or photographs of actual crew.
